## Licence (recorded 2026-10-12, B2)

The archive's own terms page (`https://data.binance.vision/terms-of-use.html`)
links `https://data.binance.vision/Binance_Vision-Terms_of_Use.pdf`,
"Binance Vision Dataset Terms", **version 1.0, last updated 2026-08-26**,
retrieved 2026-10-12 (PDF SHA-256
`94d6f61a39b5f821ef1d38f968aea1f2aa136e374f2061a2131db9a6af96bc3b`). In
summary (read the document itself for the binding text):

- §3.1: the datasets are licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.
- §3.4 and §4.1–4.4: **non-commercial use only**. Permitted uses include
  academic research and "algorithmic historical backtesting for purely
  personal non-production research". Integration into commercial financial
  products needs a separate written licence from Binance.
- §4.5: redistributed derivative works need **attribution to Binance Vision**
  and must stay under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.
- §1.4: amendments apply only to data made available after they take effect.

Consequences for this repository:

- The `klines_20m/*.csv` files are derived works. **Attribution: market data
  from Binance Vision (data.binance.vision), licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
  aggregated to 20-minute windows.** They are under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
  regardless of the repository's own licence.
- Using these files, or parameters calibrated from them, in a **commercial
  or production** deployment is not covered by these terms. Production risk
  parameters need data under a commercial licence. This is recorded in
  KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.
- For the four 2022-11 and 2026-09 files the retrieval date is unknown, so
  which version of the terms applied at download is unknown. The 2022-11
  files were re-downloaded on 2026-10-12 and are byte-identical (archive
  SHA-256 as in SOURCES.txt), so they are now also held under v1.0.

## Integrity

- `klines_20m/SOURCES.txt` lists the SHA-256 of each source archive. The
  fetch script verified each one against Binance's published `.CHECKSUM` file.
- SHA-256 of the committed CSVs, as used by the risk service and backtest, are
  recorded in `services/risk/params/calibration.json` and
  `docs/backtest/results.json` (`inputs`).

## What is not known

- **Licence of `binance_nov2022_daily.csv`.** It came from the REST API
  (`data-api.binance.vision`), not the archive. Whether the Vision Dataset
  Terms cover it was not checked; treat it as non-commercial as well.
- **Retrieval date of the 2026-09 files.** Not recorded. They were first
  committed on 2026-10-12 (commit `68663f0`); that is the commit date, not
  the retrieval date. The 2021-05, 2022-05, 2022-06 and 2022-11 archives
  carry `retrieved=` times in `klines_20m/SOURCES.txt`.
- **Retrieval date of the daily file.** The file header says 2026-10-11. This
  repository has no other record of it.
- **Venue coverage.** Binance spot only: one venue, no perpetuals, no funding,
  no cross-venue basis. Basis parameters (`basis_bps`, `basis_decorr_ppm`)
  therefore **cannot** be estimated from this data, and the risk service keeps
  their current values and says so.

## Formats

- `binance_nov2022_daily.csv`: `#` comment lines, then
  `date,sol_close,sol_low,eth_close,eth_low` (decimal USDT).
- `klines_20m/*.csv`: `window_start_ms,open,high,low,close`, prices as integer
  micro-USD (1e6). A window is 20 minutes, about one credit lifetime
  (`credit_ttl_slots` ≤ 3,000 slots).
